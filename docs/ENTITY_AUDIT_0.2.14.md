## Reviewed growth

`data/entities/growth_editorial.json` contains 35 species-specific answers, with
wiki revision IDs/hashes and sample duration/scope evidence. Three replace earlier
reviewed answers; 32 replace generic component-level growth summaries. This is
more precise guidance, not 35 previously absent topics. Counts shift from
component-backed coverage to reviewed summaries accordingly.

The earlier generic feeding suggestion was inappropriate for squid, glow squid,
polar bears and villagers. Their answers now explicitly say feeding cannot speed
growth. Other distinctions include 40-minute snifflets, separate egg/hydration
timers, Bedrock's 10-snowball ghastling growth and 10-fish nautilus growth, bees'
paused timer inside hives, turtle scutes on adulthood and small sulfur cubes
growing into large ones. Food-rate percentages are not generalized across species.
Mules can grow despite being unable to breed. Existing undead growth answers remain.

The router recognizes maturity, adulthood and accelerating babies. Food-specific
growth questions check the feeding role; carrots do not grow cows and loose
fish do not replace buckets of tropical fish for axolotls. Grammatical recipients
keep the axolotl as subject when tropical fish also match an entity name.

## Expanded combat

48 detailed summaries across 16 additional profiles: zombie, husk, drowned, both
zombie-villager catalog IDs, evoker, pillager, vindicator, wither, Ender Dragon,
pufferfish, silverfish, endermite, goat, axolotl and snow golem. The two
zombie-villager IDs share gameplay guidance; this is 16 profiles, not 16 unique mobs.

Bedrock distinctions include underwater pillager melee, crossbows not breaking,
picked-up drowned tridents not becoming ranged weapons, the wither's later-phase
dash/explosions and pufferfish Poison duration. Advice retains head/perching
restrictions for the dragon, fangs/vexes for evokers, defensive goat rams,
silverfish reinforcement prevention and non-damaging ordinary snowballs.

The compiled reviewed total rises from 1,762 to 1,842: 48 new combat topics and

component-backed summaries, 326 basic summaries, and the existing 178 variant
records / 520 variant topic answers. Source conflicts and missing loot tables
