# Normal block baseline — 0.2.22 closeout

The bounded normal block baseline is complete in the automated conversation
engine: 1,268 ordinary gameplay identifiers, 9,163 required checks, no unresolved
required slots. Actual Minecraft testing is pending. This is not exhaustive wiki
knowledge, independent proof of every fact, or every possible question wording.

## Exactly what is closed

Each in-scope block must answer identity, use, mining and acquisition. A crafting
request must yield a supported recipe, an explicitly reviewed uncraftable result,
or a clearly labeled acquisition alternative. The last category is not advertised
as a recipe. Every authored family-specific topic is also required: applicable
plant growth, oxidation/stripping, inventory, redstone, environmental behavior and
special use exceptions represented by its reviewed profile. A basic description
alone cannot satisfy the baseline. Required lists and outcomes are per subject
in BLOCK_CORE_COMPLETION.json, generated by tools/block_core_completion.mjs.

The general 15-question probe audit remains broader than this baseline. Its
unresolved advanced/non-core topics remain visible and are not converted into
invented facts or false impossibility claims. Its exhaustive-completion flag is
still false. Future targeted bugs, new content and additional wiki details can be
added without reopening this finite baseline indefinitely.

## Audience and applicability decisions

195 identifiers are explicitly classed as Education chemistry, creator tools or
internal representation blocks. The classification is an authored list/pattern in
the completion script, not inferred from unanswered questions. Their existing
availability/use answers remain available, but full chemistry recipes, command
workflows and internal-state mining are outside the normal-play baseline.

Examples of topic applicability: oxidation applies to the reviewed copper family;
plant growth applies to reviewed plant profiles; inventory applies to containers
and special holders. An unrelated block does not require every probe topic to
pass this baseline. Missing optional answers are kept as unreviewed, not silently
called impossible or fully documented.

## What this pass changed

Acquisition questions now reuse source-backed recipes where a manufactured block
has no natural-location summary. Supplemental answers cover in-world double slabs,
candle cakes, colored shulker boxes, oxidation/damage/powered states, heads, bells
and legacy wood-component recipes. All 1,463 catalog acquisition probes answer.

Reviewed fallbacks supply missing legacy wooden button/plate/trapdoor quantities,
generic bed ingredients, shulker dye recipes, sea lanterns and banners. Species
are preserved. No grid or station metadata is added to normal crafting replies.
When no supported recipe is available, the reply labels a known acquisition route
instead of claiming a recipe or falsely inferring uncraftability from absent files.

Sculk sensor redstone, ender-chest inventory/automation and sign waxing have more
specific answers. Natural sign aliases resolve before recipe-only subjects can
steal them; pronoun followups retain the selected block. New source revisions and
hashes remain in data/blocks/supplemental.json and recipe_fallbacks.json.

## Validation and next phase

The 21,945-path catalog audit, 9,163-check normal baseline and 5,780-request entity
regression matrix run in tests/builds. The focused release checklist asks only for
changed examples, not a repeat of every past test. Normal block work can move to
maintenance and items can be the next knowledge phase, with any reported block
bugs handled specifically. No multiplayer or low-end device pass is claimed.
