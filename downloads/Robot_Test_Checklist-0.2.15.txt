   Then: Can I tame it with bones?
   Expect bone taming guidance; keep the wolf as subject.
3. Can I breed wolves with fish?
   Expect No for breeding, even though fish can heal wolves in Bedrock.
   Then: Can I heal it with fish?
   Expect fish healing guidance.
4. Can a panda eat cake?
   Expect adult pandas eat cake, but cake does not breed pandas.
   Then: Can I breed it with cake?
   Expect No; use bamboo and meet nearby planted-bamboo requirements.
5. Can a fox eat chicken?
   Expect eating a carried edible item; berries are the breeding food.

   Expect inventory breeding food: 3 bread or 12 carrots/potatoes/beetroot per
   adult, or equivalent mixture; suitable spare beds are also needed.
   Then: Can it eat wheat seeds?
   Expect farmer collection/farming, not ready breeding food.
7. Can an allay eat sugar?
   Expect holding/collecting matching items, not animal feeding or breeding.
8. Can a piglin eat porkchops?
   Expect pork pickup; not bartering or breeding, and not recoverable as drops.
9. Can frogs eat small magma cubes?
   Expect small prey and froglights.
   Then: Can it eat large magma cubes?
   Expect No: only the smallest cubes can be swallowed.
10. What does a baby cow eat?
    Expect wheat guidance, without confusing feeding with taming.
