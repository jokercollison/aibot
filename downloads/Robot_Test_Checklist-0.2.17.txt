CHANGED FEATURES ONLY
The automated question matrix covers the catalog. You do not need to repeat
all old checklists or find every missing fact yourself.

1. What do creepers eat?
   Expect no taming/breeding food, not a missing-knowledge message.
   Then: How can you breed it?
   Expect: You cannot breed a creeper.
2. Can I feed an iron golem iron ingots?
   Expect repair for 25 health, distinct from breeding or taming.
3. Can I leash a creeper? / Can I bucket a bat? / Can I ride a cow?
   Expect clear normal-gameplay limitations for each action.
4. Can I milk a cow with a bucket?
   Expect milk from an adult cow; not a bucket-capture answer.
   Then ask: Can I milk a witch with a bucket?
   Expect No, not confusion with a witch drinking its own potions.
5. Does Smite work against a warden?
   Expect no extra Smite damage: a warden is not undead.
   Then ask: Does Smite work against a zombie?
   Expect the undead bonus.
6. What is a zombie?
   Then: Does a Healing potion affect it?
   Expect Healing damages undead; Harming heals them.
   Then: What about Poison?
   Expect undead Poison immunity.
7. Does Impaling work against a zombie?
   Expect Bedrock water/rain condition, not an aquatic-mobs-only rule.
8. How often does a chicken lay an egg?
   Expect adult chickens every 5-10 minutes while active, with jockey exception;
   ordinary egg laying does not require a breeding partner.
9. How many bees fit in a hive?
   Expect three and Silk Touch guidance for moving bees inside their home.
10. Why can a llama in a caravan not breed?
    Expect separate suitable tamed adults from the caravan and use hay bales.
11. What is the chance a zombie infects a villager?
    Expect Easy 0%, Normal 50%, Hard 100%, for a qualifying attacker kill.
12. How do I cure a witch?
    Expect No: the zombie-villager cure cannot reverse a witch.
13. What does an armorer sell?
    Expect a question about the level.
    Reply: master
    Expect possible enchanted diamond helmet/chestplate offers, not fixed prices.
    Then: What about novice?
    Expect novice offers, with the same profession retained.
14. What does a wandering trader sell?
    Expect its possible rotating inventory, not a claim every trader has all items.

Normal entity work is now closed out. Narrow source conflicts and command-only
oddities are documented exceptions. Report exact questions/answers for bugs;
