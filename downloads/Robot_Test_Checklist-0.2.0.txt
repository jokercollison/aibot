TIME TRAP ROBOT — TEST LIST — VERSION 0.2.0
Your reported Minecraft version: Bedrock 1.26.52

INSTALL THE UPDATE
1. Download TimeTrapRobot-0.2.0.mcaddon and open it with Minecraft.
2. Confirm BOTH packs import successfully.
3. In your test world's settings, check that BOTH updated packs are active:
   Behavior Packs: Time Trap Studios AI Bot — Behavior, version 0.2.0
   Resource Packs: Time Trap Studios AI Bot — Resources, version 0.2.0
   If old versions are active, deactivate those entries and activate the update.
4. If Minecraft still reports a missing resource pack, separately download and
   open TimeTrapRobot-Resources-0.2.0.mcpack, then
   TimeTrapRobot-Behavior-0.2.0.mcpack. Check both are active again.
   Keep your existing world; do not delete its files to fix a pack import.
5. Reopen the test world. Interact with your robot. Select Things to try for help.
   Spawn a NEW robot to test the first-meeting introduction; existing robots
   remember their owners and will not repeat it.

The original approved greeting and jokes are preserved.
The introduction now calls the robot your AI companion.
This update adds basic explanations, a namespaced TTS/AI Bot layout and a
louder sound test; it does not add
warnings for new mobs. Only creepers, nearby lava and low health are covered.

QUESTIONS TO TYPE INTO ASK
1. hello
   Expect a greeting.
2. Tell me a joke
   Expect one of the existing approved jokes.
3. How do I craft a hopper?
   Expect 5 iron ingots and 1 chest, using a crafting table.
4. What's the recipe for a hopper?
   Expect the same ingredient list as question 3.
5. How do I craft a stone pickaxe?
   Expect 3 cobblestone and 2 sticks, using a crafting table.
   It can also mention the blackstone/cobbled deepslate alternatives.
6. How do I craft a stone pick axe?
   Expect the same answer; the spaced spelling is accepted.
7. How do I make bread?
   Expect 3 wheat, using a crafting table.
8. What is a hopper?
   Expect an explanation of collecting and moving items, the spout direction,
   redstone locking, and useful automatic storage/furnace systems. It should
   explain the hopper first, then suggest a follow-up.
9. Immediately after 8: How do I craft it?
   Expect the hopper recipe again.
10. What is a creeper?
    Expect an explanation of an explosive hostile mob, plus suggested follow-ups.
11. Immediately after 10: What scares it?
    Expect cats and ocelots.
12. What scares creepers?
    Expect cats and ocelots; plural wording should work.
13. Are creepers dangerous?
    Expect an explosion warning.
14. How do I breed cows?
    Expect wheat, two adult cows, and enough room to get close.
15. What does Mending do?
    Expect experience repairing an item, highest level 1, items with durability.
16. What can I make with iron ingots?
    Expect some examples of recipes using iron ingots, not a complete list.
17. What can I make with iron?
    Expect a clarification: ingots, raw iron, or blocks?
18. Where can I find diamonds?
    Not implemented yet. Expect a friendly admission that the robot is unsure.
19. Can a cow survive lava?
    Not implemented yet. It must not invent an answer.
20. How do I craft a flibbertigibbet?
    Expect an unsure reply, not an invented recipe.

CHECK ALL ANSWERS
- No crafting grid positions, rows, symbols or shape names.
- No 'supplied data', 'registry', internal identifiers, or developer test notes.
- No 'not yet checked in-game' disclaimer.
- Ingredient quantities still need to be accurate. A stone pickaxe uses TWO sticks.
- Unknown information should get an honest, friendly answer.

ROBOT MENU AND GAMEPLAY
- Things to try: opens an in-game list of example questions.
- Settings: rename the robot; toggle warnings and owner defense.
- Follow me: walk a short distance; it should follow on ordinary terrain.
- Stay here: walk away; it should stop following. Physics can still move it.
- Set home here: set a safe home, walk away, then Return home.
  Home must be clear and loaded, and in the same dimension.
- Owner permissions: a second player should not be able to change its settings.
- Save/reload: confirm its name, owner and settings are remembered.
- Appearance: same body shape, steel-colored body, distinct cyan eyes and chest
  panel. Cyan should not wrap around the head as a diagonal band.

WARNING TESTS (KEEP YOUR TEST WORLD SAFE)
- Enable Nearby danger warnings in Settings.
- Place a creeper within about 10 blocks. Expect a creeper warning.
- Wait at least 60 seconds between warning tests; they share a cooldown.
- Stand near lava at foot level: expect a lava warning. Detection is limited.
- In Survival, with low health: expect a health warning.
- Zombies, skeletons, spiders and other mobs do NOT have proximity warnings yet.
- Owner defense is separate from spoken warnings. Test it using the settings
  toggle and a hostile mob attacking the owner. Use Creative afterward as needed.
  The Creative command is /gamemode creative (not /gamemode c).

NEW IN 0.2.0 — WHAT THINGS ARE AND DO
- What is a hopper? -> Explains collection, transfer, redstone locking and uses.
  Then: What does it do? -> Still refers to the hopper.
  Then: How do I craft it? -> 5 iron ingots and 1 chest.
- What is a furnace? -> Smelting/cooking with fuel.
- What is a chest? -> Item storage.
- What is a diamond pickaxe? -> Mining tool and harvest-tier caution.
- What are oak stairs? -> Stair-shaped building blocks.
- What is lava? -> Hot liquid, hazards and practical uses.
- What is a zombie? -> Hostile undead mob and basic behavior.
- What is a slime? -> Clarifies whether you mean the mob or block/item.
  Then ask: What is a slime mob? or What is a slime block?

Basic explanations exist for all blocks/items/entities in the imported catalog,
but not every individual mechanic is complete or independently verified.
