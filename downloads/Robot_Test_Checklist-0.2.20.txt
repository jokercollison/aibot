PLANTS AND FARMING
1. What is a cherry sapling?
   Follow up: How do I grow it? / Where can I find it?
   Expect bone meal/space guidance and cherry groves.
   How do I grow dark oak saplings?
   Expect four matching saplings in a 2-by-2 square.
2. How do I grow Nether wart?
   Expect soul sand, not soul soil; no water/light requirement; no bone meal.
   How do I grow sugar cane?
   Expect water beside soil and bone meal support.
3. How do I harvest mangrove propagules?
   Expect mature hanging propagules only; immature ones drop nothing.
   How do I harvest seagrass? / How do I harvest hanging roots?
   Expect shears for seagrass; shears or Silk Touch for hanging roots.
4. How do I grow small dripleaf?
   Expect bone meal makes big dripleaf, not more small dripleaves.
   How do I harvest glow berries?
   Expect use a berry-bearing vine; no Fortune increase.
5. How do I harvest brain coral blocks?
   Expect Silk Touch pickaxe for live blocks.
   What happens with water and dead brain coral?
   Expect water does not revive dead coral.

STORAGE, WORKSTATIONS AND REDSTONE
6. What is a hopper?
   Follow up: Does it need redstone power?
   Expect power locks/stops the hopper; it runs unpowered.
7. How do I mine blue shulker boxes?
   Expect contents remain inside the dropped box.
   How many slots does a barrel hold?
   Expect 27.
8. How do I use a blast furnace? / How do I use a smoker?
   Expect restricted smelting versus food cooking.
   How do I use a fletching table?
   Expect fletcher job site; no player crafting interface.
9. How does redstone affect a copper bulb?
   Expect toggles once per new activation and keeps its state.
   How does redstone affect a redstone lamp?
   Expect lit while powered, rather than permanent toggle.
10. How do I mine copper doors? / How do I mine copper trapdoors?
    Expect doors collectible by hand; trapdoors need stone pickaxe or better.
    How do I use iron doors? / How do I use copper doors?
    Expect iron redstone-only; copper also opens by hand.

LIGHTING, TRANSPORT AND SPECIAL BLOCKS
11. How do I mine a campfire? / How do I mine a soul campfire?
    Expect Silk Touch for the block; otherwise charcoal versus soul soil.
    How do I mine a chiseled bookshelf?
    Expect Silk Touch for the block, stored books drop separately.
12. How do I use powered rails? / How do I use detector rails?
    Expect acceleration/braking versus a cart sensor.
    How do I use activator rails?
    Expect special cart actions, not acceleration.
13. How do I use a beacon? / How do I use a conduit?
    Expect mineral pyramid/clear beam versus water/prismarine frame.
14. Is a bed dangerous? / Is a respawn anchor dangerous?
    Expect bed explosions in Nether/End; charged anchor explosions outside Nether.
    How do I mine a monster spawner? / How do I mine a vault?
    Expect no collectible block, even with Silk Touch.
15. How do I mine copper bars? / How do I mine copper chains?
    Expect any pickaxe, unlike copper blocks needing stone or better.
    How do I mine oak double slabs?
    Expect two slabs, not a separate double-slab item.
