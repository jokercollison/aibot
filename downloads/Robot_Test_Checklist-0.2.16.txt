REPRESENTATIVE CHECKS ONLY
The automated matrix already exercises 2,992 questions. You do not need to
repeat the whole catalog or discover every missing fact.

1. How can you breed a creeper?
   Expect: You cannot breed a creeper.
   Then: What does it eat?
   Expect an honest not-sure answer, not invented feeding advice.
   Then: How can you move it?
   Expect a minecart route; it can still be dangerous and a cart alone does not
   guarantee persistence in Bedrock.
2. How long does a baby creeper take to grow?
   Expect no normal baby form, not a made-up 20-minute timer.
3. How long does a baby piglin take to grow?
   Expect baby piglins do not become adults.
4. How do small slimes grow?
   Expect size variants, not babies that become large by waiting or feeding.
5. How does a happy ghast attack?
   Expect a passive mount, not hostile-ghast fireballs.
6. How does a zombie horse attack?
   Expect the zombie rider and passive mount to be distinguished.
7. How does a sulfur cube attack?
   Expect ordinarily passive behavior with absorbed-block hazards.
8. Does a cow despawn?
   Expect naturally spawned cows can distance-despawn unless persistent;
   naming or suitable supported interactions can help keep one.
9. Does a copper golem despawn?
   Expect ordinary persistence, with oxidation/statue transformation distinct.
10. How can you move an iron golem?
    Expect a lead and a safe route.
11. How can you move a ghast?
    Expect minecart handling with danger and persistence cautions.
12. How can you move a warden?
    Expect no ordinary lead/minecart transport; luring is dangerous, not taming.

Keep using one conversation for follow-ups. Things to try, approved texture,
chirp and earlier knowledge remain available. Report exact unexpected questions
and answers. There are known unsupported questions and partial cases; this is
an entity baseline, not a promise to answer everything. Multiplayer and actual
phone/console performance still require separate testing.
