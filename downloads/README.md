Use a **fresh Creative test world**, activate ONLY the Behavior **0.2.5** and
Resources **0.2.5** pair, and spawn a new robot. Both pack names and descriptions
show 0.2.5; the robot menu must show **v0.2.5**. Summon ID:
`tts_ai_bot_v0_2_5:robot`.

This release has fresh UUIDs for every pack header/module and version-specific
custom content names/paths. Old worlds and robots remain separate. Things to try
is retained, and crafting answers give ingredient amounts only. The working 0.1.1
texture and cube mapping are preserved in the actual packaged resources.

Earlier version files here are historical builds. Do not use them for this test.
Actual Minecraft appearance, sound, loading and multiplayer still require testing.

The 0.2.5 .mcaddon contains normal behavior/resource pack folders directly at its root. The standalone .mcpack links above are optional separate imports; they are NOT inside the addon.

This build includes reviewed mining-location guidance for nine ore/resource groups and Bedrock Slime spawning. The cached wiki reference collection is not bundled and most wiki topics are not yet connected to answers.

Choose **Talk to me** once, then keep sending messages. Answers and recent conversation appear above the next input. Close/Back/Escape stops the conversation. A brief form refresh after Send is expected.
