Optional summon: /summon tts_ai_bot_v0_2_8:robot

FOOD PARSING AND DIRECT NEGATIVE ANSWERS
- Can I tame a wolf with beef?
  Expect a direct No, bones for taming, and food uses after taming.
- Can I tame a wolf with a diamond block?
  Expect No and bones, not a description of diamond blocks.
- What do chickens eat?
  Expect suitable seeds/pitcher pods, not confusion with chicken meat.
- What do rabbits eat?
  Expect carrots, golden carrots or dandelions.
- What do cows eat when they are 200 years old and in the plains?
  Expect wheat. Incidental wording must not hide the food question.

CONDITIONAL DROPS (KEEP USING THE SAME CONVERSATION)
1. What does a wither skeleton drop when I kill it with Looting III?
   Expect coal/bones and a conditional 8.5% skull chance, not a guaranteed skull.
2. What does an adult cow drop while burning with Looting III?
   Expect possible 0-5 leather and 1-6 steak, not raw beef.
3. What does a blaze drop when killed by a skeleton?
   Expect no eligible ordinary rod drop; rods require a player/tamed-wolf kill.
4. What does a creeper drop when a wither skeleton kills it?
   Expect the CREEPER to remain the subject; gunpowder is possible.
   A wither skeleton is not one of the supported disc-killer conditions.
5. What does a creeper drop when killed by a skeleton?
   Expect gunpowder and one eligible music disc, not every disc.
6. What does a creeper drop when killed by a charged creeper?
   Expect the separate creeper-head condition.
7. What does a baby zombie riding a chicken drop when I kill it without Looting?
   Expect conditional Lava Chicken disc and applicable zombie loot.
8. What does a sheared sheep drop without Looting?
   Then: What does an unsheared sheep drop without Looting?
   Expect wool only for the unsheared form; meat has its own rules.
9. What does a wither skeleton drop?
   Then: with Looting III
   Expect the same subject with Looting applied.

BROADER ENTITY ANSWERS
- Can I tame a creeper? -> No.
- Can I breed a warden? -> No.
- How do I breed sulfur cubes? -> Splitting, not food-based pairing.
- Are rabbits dangerous? -> Ordinary Bedrock rabbits are passive; killer bunny is Java-only.
- What is an elder guardian ghost? Then: Where does it spawn?
  -> Technical entity; commands, not ordinary monument spawning.
- What is a spider jockey? -> Separate mount/rider, with Bedrock variants.
