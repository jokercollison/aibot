EXPERIENCE AND EQUIPMENT
1. How much XP does a baby piglin drop?
   Expect base 1 XP in Bedrock, not Java’s 5.
2. How much XP does a baby hoglin drop?
   Expect 5; ordinary baby-animal zero-XP rules do not apply.
3. How much XP does the Ender Dragon drop?
   Expect 12,000 first defeat; 500 after resummoning. Points, not levels.
4. How much experience does an evoker drop when killed by an iron golem?
   Expect the EVOKER’s 10 XP regardless of death method in Bedrock.
5. How much XP does an axolotl drop?
   Expect honest uncertainty about the exact number; conflicting sources remain.
6. Does a vex drop its sword with Looting III?
   Expect No; Looting does not change it.
7. Does a drowned drop its trident with Looting III?
   Expect equipment/kill conditions and Bedrock 25% base, 28% with Looting III.

COMBAT AND FOLLOW-UPS
8. What is a warden?

   Expect sonic boom; ordinary shields/armor/walls do not stop that attack.

   Expect damage can be blocked but Levitation still applies in Bedrock.

    Expect gold armor and gold offerings do not pacify it.
11. How do I defeat a breeze?
    Expect projectile deflection, with melee/wind-charge counterplay.
12. How do I defeat a creaking?
    Expect watch it and locate/break its linked heart; command-created differs.
13. How does a cave spider attack?
    Expect Poison on Normal/Hard, not Easy.
14. Why is an iron golem attacking me?
    Expect natural-village aggression versus player-built golems not attacking players.
